### Bias-search-

A dedicated fan application where you can explore profile information, filmographies, and fun updates about all of our favorite, most-loved actors (**our biases**)! 

### ✨ Features

* **Bias Profiles:** Complete profiles, fun facts, and background info on the actors we love.
* **Search Function:** Quickly look up your favorite celebrity to see their latest projects.
* **Photo Gallery:** A collection of high-quality photos and moments from their dramas and movies.
